If you cannot clearly describe your business in one sentence, your marketing message will likely feel inconsistent. This simple framework helps small businesses sharpen clarity fast.

## One-sentence framework

Use this structure:

"We help [audience] solve [problem] so they can [outcome]."

## Example

"We help Australian small business owners clarify their brand and marketing strategy so they can grow with confidence."

## How to improve your sentence

- Remove jargon.
- Keep it specific.
- Focus on customer outcome, not internal process.

- One sentence drafted.
- Team can repeat it consistently.
- Sentence appears on core pages.
- Customer outcome is clear.
- Language is simple and specific.

## FAQ

### How long should this sentence be?

Aim for one short sentence that is clear in under 15 seconds.

### Should we mention every service in it?

No. Focus on the primary problem and core outcome.

### When should we update it?

Update when your audience focus or core offer changes.
