AI is now part of daily marketing workflows. The strongest brands treat AI as a helper, not a replacement for their voice.

## Keep Your Strategy Human-First

1. Set your tone, values, and non-negotiables before generating content.
2. Review every output for clarity and relevance.
3. Prioritize consistency over volume.

The goal is not to sound automated faster. The goal is to communicate more clearly, more consistently, and more helpfully.

## FAQ

### Is AI content bad for brand trust?

Not by default. Trust usually drops only when content is unreviewed, generic, or inconsistent with brand voice.

### What should humans keep ownership of?

Positioning, messaging decisions, and final publishing approval should remain human-led.

### Can small businesses use AI without large teams?

Yes. A lightweight workflow with clear templates and review steps is enough to start.
