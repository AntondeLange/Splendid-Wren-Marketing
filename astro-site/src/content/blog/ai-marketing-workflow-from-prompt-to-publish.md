A reliable AI marketing workflow should reduce production time while improving consistency. This framework gives small businesses a practical sequence from prompt to publish.

## 1) Plan the objective

Define who the content is for, what action you want, and where it will be published.

## 2) Use a structured prompt

Include tone, audience, format, and key points. Save templates so the team can reuse the same quality baseline.

## 3) Draft with AI

Generate a first draft quickly, then focus your effort on review and refinement.

## 4) Human review for quality

Check facts, clarity, brand voice, and customer relevance before publishing.

For AI implementation support, see [AI Marketing Support for Small Business](/ai-marketing-support-small-business).

## 5) Publish with clear internal links

Link each piece to your service pages and contact path.

## Workflow Checklist

- Objective defined before prompting.
- Prompt template selected from a shared library.
- Draft reviewed for voice and accuracy.
- Internal links added to service + contact pages.
- Performance reviewed weekly.

## FAQ

### How long should this workflow take each week?

Most small teams can run it in two focused sessions per week.

### Should AI create final copy without review?

No. Human review is essential for quality and brand consistency.

### What should we measure first?

Track enquiry-driven outcomes such as qualified leads and form submissions.
